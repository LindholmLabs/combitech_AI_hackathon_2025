# Minesweeper solver from combitech AI hackathon 2025
Minesweeper solver, second place finish at AI hackathon arranged by Combitech Skövde in 2025.
Solves Expert level (16x30, 99 mines) difficulty on [https://minesweeperonline.com/](https://minesweeperonline.com/) in under 2 seconds.
Hackathon took place over 4 hours of development time.

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> As this was an AI hackathon where the goal was to try new AI agents, this project was developed entirely with GPT-5-Codex.
